## What changed

No new law — the reminder is that Kuwait's commercial agency framework remains a binding constraint for many product categories. A foreign supplier that wants to sell in Kuwait may need a registered Kuwaiti agent or distributor, and the agency registration can give the agent control over the import and sale of the product.

The practical effect is that the freight decision comes after the agency decision, not before it.

## Why it matters

An agency agreement can determine:

- **Who is the importer of record.** Often the agent, not the end customer.
- **Who holds the product registration.** The agent may hold the KUCAS certificate or the product registration in its name.
- **Who controls the price and the channel.** The agent may have exclusive rights.
- **How difficult it is to exit.** Agency registrations can survive the commercial relationship.

If the agency is not settled, a shipment can arrive with no valid importer, or with an importer that the supplier did not choose.

## Who is affected

- Foreign suppliers entering Kuwait for the first time.
- Brands that want to change distributors.
- Ecommerce sellers that want to sell directly.
- Companies responding to a Kuwaiti government tender.

## What to do

1. **Check the category.** Does the product require a registered agent?
2. **Confirm the registration.** Is the agency registered with the Ministry of Commerce and Industry?
3. **Map the import file.** Who is the importer, who holds the product certificate, who pays duty and VAT (currently no VAT)?
4. **Negotiate the exit.** Term, transfer of registration, stock buy-back.
5. **Price the commission.** The agent's margin is part of the landed cost.
6. **Take legal advice.** Kuwaiti agency law is specific; a generic distribution agreement may not be enough.

## Our read

Kuwait is a relationship market, and the agency is the relationship. A supplier that treats the agent as a freight forwarder will discover that the agent controls the market access.

The right sequence is: choose the agent, register the agency, register the product, then book the freight. Reversing the sequence is expensive.
