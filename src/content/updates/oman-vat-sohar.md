## What changed

Oman's VAT rate has been 5% since April 2021. The rate is stable, and it is the lowest in the GCC after Qatar and Kuwait's zero rates. The more important change for shippers is structural: Sohar has become a credible northern gateway for cargo that wants to avoid the Strait of Hormuz or serve the UAE's northern emirates.

That makes the VAT rate only one line in a larger calculation.

## Why it matters

A Sohar import has a different cost structure from a Jebel Ali import:

- **VAT:** 5% on CIF plus duty, recoverable by a registered Omani importer.
- **Duty:** usually 5%.
- **Inland:** Sohar to Muscat is about 200 km; Sohar to Dubai is about 200 km plus the UAE border.
- **Border:** the Al Buraimi crossing adds customs and a truck leg.
- **Free zone:** Sohar Freezone can defer duty and VAT for goods that remain in the zone.

For an Oman-bound consignee, the 5% VAT is a cash-flow cost, not a permanent cost, if the importer is registered. For a UAE-bound consignee using Sohar as a back door, the Omani VAT should not apply to genuine transit cargo, but the documentation must support the status.

## Who is affected

- Oman importers in Muscat and the Batinah coast.
- UAE importers using Sohar when Jebel Ali is congested.
- Free-zone tenants in Sohar.
- Project contractors in Duqm and the northern industrial belt.

## What to do

1. **Confirm the importer.** An Omani entity with a commercial registration and customs account.
2. **Decide the status.** Oman import, free-zone stock or transit to the UAE.
3. **Price the land leg.** Sohar–Muscat and Sohar–Dubai are different costs.
4. **Check the border.** The Al Buraimi crossing can queue; build a buffer.
5. **Confirm the product certificate.** Oman's standards apply, plus sector requirements.

## Our read

Oman's 5% VAT is a genuine advantage, but it is not the reason to use Sohar. The reason is the Hormuz bypass and the northern hinterland. If the cargo is UAE-bound, the border and the truck usually erase the VAT saving.

For an Oman-bound importer, Sohar is a good gateway: short land leg, low VAT, a free zone and a stable currency. The constraint is the smaller service board, not the tax.
