## Direct answer

Jebel Ali is not a normal container gateway in November 2026. Direct liner calls are ad hoc; most boxes discharge at [Khor Fakkan](/ports/sharjah/) or Fujairah and arrive by truck. Use Jebel Ali only with a confirmed landbridge, a named trucker and 12–15 days of port-to-door time built into your plan. *(Editorial judgment)*

## What changed in the last 60 days

**13–19 October 2026.** Gemini relaunched its Asia–Middle East loop and replaced the Jebel Ali call with [Khor Fakkan](/ports/sharjah/). The rotation was redrawn around an east-coast port, so the carrier's schedule, slot allocation and empty-container flow now assume Jebel Ali is not the first call. *(Reported fact)*

**30 October 2026.** Kuehne+Nagel put average vessel waits at 7.25 days at Khor Fakkan and 8.86 at Fujairah. Container transfers from those ports to Jebel Ali, Sharjah and [Abu Dhabi](/ports/khalifa/), previously about five days, were taking 12–15 days. Maersk imposed temporary booking restrictions on Khor Fakkan, Fujairah and selected Upper Gulf destinations. Splash247, citing Linerlytica, called Middle East container congestion a record and said Jebel Ali remains "largely restricted to ad hoc vessel calls." *(Reported fact)*

**3 November 2026.** Fujairah resumed oil loadings after an attack. That helps energy flows but does not restore container rotations; tanker and liner traffic are recovering on different clocks. *(Reported fact)*

Jebel Ali's infrastructure has not been destroyed. No terminal damage was confirmed from the 5 August industrial-area explosions, and the port still handles ad hoc calls. Yet throughput fell 90.1% in the second quarter, and DP World has spent about USD 100 million a month keeping a largely dormant port ready. A port can be physically intact and commercially idle at the same time. *(Reported fact; interpretation editorial)*

## Why it matters

Jebel Ali anchors [JAFZA](/countries/uae/)'s 11,000+ companies, Dubai's customs and trucking network, and transshipment to Qatar, Kuwait and Iraq. When vessel calls stop, the system stretches rather than stops. JAFZA tenants still get cargo by truck from Khor Fakkan or Fujairah, with a week added to lead times. Transshipment has moved to east-coast ports and Oman, and it does not return on a ceasefire. Exporters face empty boxes in the wrong place, and consignees absorb storage and demurrage when a box is declared at another entry point. *(Editorial judgment)*

## How to act: five steps before you book

**1. Confirm the actual discharge port in writing.** Do not accept "Jebel Ali" on a quotation without a carrier confirmation that the vessel calls Jebel Ali. Ask for the vessel, rotation and discharge terminal. If the answer is "Jebel Ali via landbridge," treat it as a Khor Fakkan or Fujairah booking with an inland leg. *(Editorial judgment)*

**2. Use a through B/L or a written land-leg addendum.** It should name the discharge port, inland destination, trucker and free time. If the forwarder issues a port-to-port B/L to Khor Fakkan and a separate trucking quote, you own the gap. *(Industry claim)*

**3. File [customs clearance](/guides/customs-clearance/) at the actual entry point.** A Dubai Customs declaration for Jebel Ali will not match a container discharged at Khor Fakkan. The importer needs a declaration at the port of entry, plus a transit or free-zone transfer for JAFZA. This is the most common cause of a 7–14 day delay. *(Industry claim)*

**4. Pre-arrange the empty return.** Ask where the empty goes, who pays interchange, and what happens if no slot is available. A box returnable only at Jebel Ali can incur detention before it reaches your warehouse. *(Industry claim)*

**5. Do not use the port yard as storage.** In a congested market, a box left in the yard can cost more than the ocean freight. Book a JAFZA 3PL before the vessel arrives. *(Editorial judgment)*

**Transit reality.** A box that once moved from Jebel Ali's berth to a Dubai warehouse in a day now moves from Khor Fakkan's berth in roughly 12–15 days, including waiting and trucking. Add a day for Abu Dhabi and border formalities for [Saudi Arabia](/countries/saudi-arabia/). This is not a reason to avoid Jebel Ali altogether, but it is a reason to stop pricing it as a port-to-door product. *(Reported fact + editorial judgment)*

## Terminal and hinterland

Jebel Ali opened in 1979 and covers 134.68 square kilometres, the largest man-made harbour in the world. Terminal 1 alone has 10 berths, a 3,242-metre quay, 133.34 hectares and 37 quay cranes. Terminals 2 and 3 add deep-water capacity for larger ships; in normal times the port markets 80+ weekly services to more than 150 ports. *(Reported fact)*

It sits 35 kilometres southwest of central Dubai, next to JAFZA and Sheikh Zayed Road. Abu Dhabi is 140 kilometres away, Sharjah 40, the Saudi border at Ghuwaifat 380, and the Omani border 180. That geography makes it a distribution hub for the UAE and a staging point for Saudi Arabia, Oman and the Upper Gulf. *(Reported fact + editorial judgment)*

## Clearance: how cargo actually clears at Jebel Ali

Jebel Ali clearance runs through Dubai Customs and its Mirsal 2 declaration system. The importer of record needs a UAE trade licence, a customs client code and a licence activity covering the goods. The declaration captures HS code, customs value, origin and Incoterm, and triggers duty and VAT assessment. *(Industry claim)*

The documents are standard; the failure points are not:

- **Importer mismatch.** The B/L consignee must match the trade licence and customs code. A post-sailing switch requires a B/L amendment, which costs time and money.
- **HS code mismatch.** A wrong code invites inspection, reclassification and duty reassessment. For anti-dumping-sensitive goods such as [car batteries](/sectors/batteries/), ceramic tiles and hydraulic cement, the difference can be tens of thousands of dollars.
- **Missing certificates.** [ECAS](/guides/certification/), TDRA, Dubai Municipality food registration and MOHAP registrations are checked at clearance. "In progress" is not a certificate.
- **Valuation challenge.** A declared value below the customs benchmark triggers a query. Keep the invoice, payment proof and a market benchmark.

*(Industry claim)*

Once assessed, the container is released to the importer or moved under a transfer declaration into JAFZA. JAFZA has its own customs office and gate regime. Goods in the free zone are not subject to duty or import VAT until they leave for the mainland; that transfer is a separate declaration and payment. *(Industry claim)*

**Free time is the number to negotiate.** Standard free periods are short and demurrage starts quickly. When vessels wait a week or more, free time matters more than a few dollars on the ocean rate. Get it in writing, including the storage tariff and free days at the actual discharge port. *(Editorial judgment)*

## The landbridge, explained

The landbridge is a workaround, not a product. A carrier books cargo to Jebel Ali, discharges at Khor Fakkan or Fujairah, and trucks it to Jebel Ali under a through B/L or a separate land-leg contract. Maersk began booking Jebel Ali via landbridge in August 2026; other carriers followed with their own variants. *(Reported fact)*

There are three models: carrier through B/L, forwarder door-delivery, and merchant haulage. The landbridge adds 12–15 days from discharge to Jebel Ali, Sharjah or Abu Dhabi. It also adds a customs question: goods are imported at the east-coast port and moved inland. If the move is under transit, the declaration must be closed at destination; if it is a free-zone transfer, the JAFZA gate must accept the container. A mismatch between entry declaration and inland destination is the most common landbridge failure. *(Industry claim)*

## Which cargo should still use Jebel Ali

Jebel Ali remains right for JAFZA-bound inventory that will be stored or redistributed, project and breakbulk cargo needing heavy-lift or Ro-Ro equipment, and reefer cargo needing plug-in capacity and a Dubai cold-chain 3PL. It also suits Dubai south cargo. For consumer goods, [e-commerce](/sectors/ecommerce/) replenishment, [electronics](/sectors/electronics/), furniture and machinery for Abu Dhabi, east-coast routing is faster. For Saudi-bound cargo, compare [Jeddah](/ports/jeddah/) or [Dammam](/ports/dammam/) direct; for northern UAE, [Sohar](/ports/sohar/) is the overland alternative. *(Editorial judgment)*

## Risk and compliance

**Physical risk.** The 5 August 2026 explosions in the Jebel Ali industrial area showed the port sits inside a conflict zone. No terminal damage was confirmed, but insurers price the risk, not the report. A recurrence could close the port or roads with little notice. *(Reported fact + editorial judgment)*

**War-risk insurance.** Standard cargo policies exclude war, strikes and terrorism unless endorsed. Confirm cover for the actual discharge port, the landbridge and the inland warehouse. A policy ending at Jebel Ali's port limit does not cover a container discharged at Khor Fakkan. *(Industry claim)*

**Carrier force majeure and sanctions.** Carriers have changed discharge ports, suspended bookings and imposed congestion surcharges; the contract should say who pays for a carrier-initiated diversion. Separately, the UAE suspended trade with Iran in August 2026, so screen counterparties and end-use before booking re-export cargo. *(Editorial judgment + reported fact)*

**Cargo security and abandonment.** A container that is not cleared can be abandoned and sold by the port authority. With 12–15 day transfers, track the box at the discharge port, not at Jebel Ali. *(Industry claim)*

## Our read

**Jebel Ali is a landbridge destination, not a vessel gateway.** Book it only when the consignee is JAFZA-based, the cargo needs breakbulk or reefer equipment, or the landbridge is explicitly priced and contracted — see the [direct-versus-landbridge comparison](/compare/jebel-ali-vs-direct/). For general Dubai-bound cargo, Khor Fakkan plus truck is the better default. *(Editorial judgment)*

**Do not store cargo at the port, and do not sign a long JAFZA lease on a 2027 recovery bet.** Use a 3PL with a known gate and trucking partner, and start with flexible space. Port storage is a demurrage machine in a congested market. *(Editorial judgment)*

**What would change our mind.** Weekly liner calls at Jebel Ali for eight consecutive weeks, Khor Fakkan waits below three days, and a stable war-risk insurance market. Until then, treat Jebel Ali as a trucked destination. *(Editorial judgment)*

## FAQ

**Is Jebel Ali Port operating?**
Yes, but not as a normal container gateway. It handles ad hoc vessel calls, and cargo reaches Jebel Ali by landbridge from Khor Fakkan and Fujairah. The infrastructure is intact; the liner network is not. *(Reported fact)*

**Can I book an FCL directly to Jebel Ali?**
Only if the carrier confirms a vessel call at Jebel Ali for that service. Many quotes that say "Jebel Ali" are landbridge quotes with a discharge at Khor Fakkan or Fujairah. Ask for the vessel, rotation and discharge terminal in writing. *(Editorial judgment)*

**How long does clearance take?**
Two to five working days when documents are clean; one to three weeks if the HS code is wrong, the consignee does not match the licence, or a certificate is missing. File at the actual port of entry, not at Jebel Ali. *(Industry claim)*

**What is the Jebel Ali landbridge?**
A carrier or forwarder arrangement in which cargo is discharged at Khor Fakkan or Fujairah and trucked to Jebel Ali. Maersk has offered Jebel Ali bookings via landbridge since August 2026. It adds 12–15 days from discharge to Jebel Ali, Sharjah or Abu Dhabi. *(Reported fact)*

**Is JAFZA still worth using?**
Yes, if you need duty and VAT deferral, regional distribution or a Dubai address. JAFZA has 11,000+ companies and remains open. Model the slower inbound leg before signing a lease. *(Reported fact + editorial judgment)*

**What happens if the port is attacked again?**
Expect a temporary closure, carrier suspensions and a scramble for trucks. The response is the same as today: discharge at Khor Fakkan or Fujairah, clear at the actual entry point, and truck to a pre-arranged 3PL. For urgent cargo, compare the [sea-versus-air trade-off](/compare/sea-vs-air/) before switching modes. *(Editorial judgment)*
